Good questions

Straight answers, including the limits.

Which files can I upload?

JPG, PNG and PDF, up to 20 MB each. Several files can be processed in one go.

Is my data private?

Documents are processed on the server you run DocuMind on. Signed-in scans are stored under your account only; nothing is stored for the live demo above.

How accurate is it?

The model scores about 94% F1 on held-out invoice layouts from FATURA, a synthetic dataset. Real invoices vary, so every field shows a confidence score and the table is checked against the printed totals for you to review.

What can I export?

CSV (one row per document, or one row per line item), JSON, and your whole dataset as a zip with the original images.

Every scan becomes your dataset

Create an account and your scans, images and extracted fields are stored together and exportable as one archive.
